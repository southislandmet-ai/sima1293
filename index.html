Paste your written risk levels, e.g. “Enhanced risk of heavy rain and snow above 500 m for Westland, Fiordland and the Southern Alps; slight risk of severe southerly gales along the Canterbury and Otago coasts”. Claude asks anything it needs, confirms its understanding, and plots the areas when you press Plot on map.
